- Jurisdiction: Great Britain (HSE)
- Search evidence: HSE’s completed health-and-safety training competence search defines competence as the combination of training, skills, experience and knowledge, and the ability to apply these to perform a task safely.

## Managing contractors

- Publisher: Health and Safety Executive
- URL: https://www.hse.gov.uk/workplacetransport/personnel/managingcontractors.htm
- Jurisdiction: Great Britain (HSE)
- Search evidence: HSE’s completed contractor-management search says employers should choose and assess contractors as carefully as permanent staff, including checking certificates and qualifications are valid and up to date.
