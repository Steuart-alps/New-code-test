# Tree work

- ### [New to health and safety?](https://www.hse.gov.uk/treework/newto.htm)



Introduction to tree work

- ### [Safety hazards](https://www.hse.gov.uk/treework/safety-topics/index.htm)



Aerial work, chainsaws, machinery, working at height, vehicles

- ### [Forestry and arboriculture workers](https://www.hse.gov.uk/treework/areyou/index.htm)



Tree surgeons, chainsaw operators, forestry workers and managers

- ### [Resources](https://www.hse.gov.uk/treework/resources/index.htm)



Arboriculture and Forestry Advisory Group (AFAG) guidance, case studies, research

- ### [Site management](https://www.hse.gov.uk/treework/site-management/index.htm)



Training, lone working, public safety, first aid, traffic management

- ### [Health topics](https://www.hse.gov.uk/treework/health/index.htm)

Effective training, continuous professional development (CPD)
