---
name: AnyTrack roadmap
description: Product decision to defer user-configurable tracks while the main compliance tracks are prioritised.
---

User-configurable “AnyTrack” remains a future product direction. Work should currently focus on completing, stabilising and improving the existing main tracks first.

**Why:** The main tracks still have higher-priority functional and consistency work, and AnyTrack would introduce a broader configurable-track engine rather than a small isolated feature.

**How to apply:** Do not implement or prioritise AnyTrack unless the user explicitly brings it back. Keep the idea available for later, including configurable checklists, dashboard status, actions, reminders, reporting and track-specific manager routing.
