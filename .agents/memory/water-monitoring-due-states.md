---
name: Water monitoring due states
description: Safety decision about risk-assessed site monitoring frequencies and material-change review.
---

Water, pool and spa due dates must come from the approved controls for the selected site. A missing plan or one flagged after a material change is a review-required state, not a reason to substitute template intervals. Apply this consistently to module pages, the main dashboard and reminder calculations.

**Why:** A generic schedule can falsely tell managers a site is up to date when its competent person has not assessed the installation or revised the written scheme after a change.

**How to apply:** When extending water safety features, scope records and approved frequencies to the same tenant and site; never infer approval from an older template profile or from a recorded check alone. Preserve failed observations as actionable independently of the monitoring schedule.
